About Our Institution

Rooted in Tradition, Committed to Progress

Islamia Public School Kuro (Regd) was established in 1998 to bridge educational gaps in Village Kuro and surrounding areas of Tehsil Ghowari, District Ghanche, Gilgit-Baltistan. For over two decades, the school has operated as a foundational hub for academic discipline, moral growth, and modern technological readiness.

Academic Rigor

Comprehensive curriculum designed to develop critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and strong core foundations in STEM and languages.

Character Building

Emphasis on moral integrity, mutual respect, civic responsibility, and traditional cultural ethics alongside formal academic learning.

Digital Literacy

Early exposure to desktop computers, basic IT concepts, and digital skills to prepare students for a technology-driven world.

Community Empowerment

A locally driven educational model encouraging parental involvement, regional teacher development, and accessible youth schooling.
