Our Story & Family

Built by the Community, for Our Children

Islamia Public School Kuro wasn’t built as an enterprise—it was created out of a warm vision shared by parents and elders in Village Kuro. Since 1998, we have worked hand in hand with local families to give every child in Ghanche a safe, encouraging space to learn, read, laugh, and prepare for bright futures.

Individual Care

Every child is known by name. Our dedicated teachers treat every student like family, encouraging them step by step.

Moral & Cultural Ethics

Instilling respect, humility, Islamic values, and rich Balti cultural traditions that ground our students throughout life.

Practical Computer Skills

Bringing modern computer knowledge into our village, helping local youth confidently embrace technology.

Open Parent Dialogue

We welcome parents into our campus anytime. Education is a joint journey between school and home.
